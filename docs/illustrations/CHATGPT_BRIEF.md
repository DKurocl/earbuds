# Settings illustrations: brief for an image model

The app's settings page has a card at the top that shows what a setting does: an animation, as Pixel's
Settings pages do. The app animates the art itself (nodding, lifting, popping in), so the model draws
the **parts** of each scene, each on its own, and the app puts them together and moves them.

Paste the **Style** block once, then one scene at a time. Ask for a PNG with a transparent background.
If the background comes out white or checkered, ask again: "transparent background, PNG, alpha".

## Style (paste first)

```
I need flat illustrations in the style of Google Pixel's Settings app (Android 16, Material 3
Expressive): the small animated pictures at the top of Pixel settings pages such as "Adaptive Sound",
"Spatial audio" or "Quick Tap".

Rules for every image:
- Flat 2D vector look. Soft, rounded, friendly geometry. No outlines, no strokes around shapes.
- At most one very soft gradient per shape. Shadows only as a flat, slightly darker shape.
- Palette (use only these): blue #4285F4, light blue #AECBFA, pale blue #D2E3FC, lilac #C58AF9,
  pale lilac #E9D2FD, green #34A853, pale green #CEEAD6, warm grey #5F6368, light grey #DADCE0,
  white #FFFFFF, skin tones #F6C9A8 and #C68863.
- People: simple, faceless (no eyes, nose or mouth), head and shoulders only, short hair as one
  shape, calm posture. Pixel's illustration people, not cartoons.
- The earbuds are Sony WF-1000XM5: a rounded pebble-shaped body in silver-white (#E8E8E6 with a
  #C9C9C5 shade), a soft grey foam ear tip, and a small round gold microphone grille (#B89B5E) on the
  outer side. The case is a smooth wide pebble, silver-white, its lid a little shorter than its base,
  a thin seam between, one tiny light on the front. No logos, no text anywhere.
- Transparent background (PNG with alpha). Nothing touches the edges; a margin around every part.
- When I ask for "parts", draw each part separately, side by side with clear space between them,
  all at the same scale, so they can be cut out and animated one by one.
```

## Scenes

Each scene: the finished picture first (so we see the composition), then its parts.

1. **Speak-to-Chat**
   ```
   Scene: a person (head and shoulders, side view facing left) wearing the earbud starts to talk;
   a rounded speech bubble with three dots comes out of their mouth; next to their ear, small music
   notes that are fading. Wide picture, 2.4:1.
   Then parts, same scale, transparent: (a) the person with the earbud in; (b) the speech bubble
   with three dots; (c) three single music notes; (d) a round blue pause badge.
   ```
2. **DSEE (restores detail)**
   ```
   Scene: a sound wave drawn as a thick rounded line that is jagged and blocky on the right and
   smooth and detailed on the left; between them a soft glowing vertical bar with a small sparkle.
   2.4:1. Parts: (a) the blocky wave alone; (b) the smooth wave alone, same length and position;
   (c) the bar with its sparkle.
   ```
3. **Hear my voice in calls**
   ```
   Scene: a person in profile on a call, a small arc of soft dots goes from their mouth round to the
   earbud in their ear. 2.4:1. Parts: (a) the person with the earbud; (b) one soft dot; (c) a small
   voice-wave badge.
   ```
4. **Touch controls**
   ```
   Scene: a close-up of one earbud, a fingertip tapping its outer side, two soft circles of ripple
   around the tap. 2.4:1. Parts: (a) the earbud alone, three-quarter view, tip to the left; (b) the
   fingertip (index finger, from the right); (c) one ripple ring.
   ```
5. **Noise control cycle**
   ```
   Scene: three rounded badges in a row, joined by a dotted path: an ear with sound blocked, an ear
   with sound coming in, and an ear with nothing on. The middle one is bigger and blue. 2.4:1.
   Parts: each badge alone, in a grey version and a blue version.
   ```
6. **Head gestures**
   ```
   Scene: a person facing us wearing both earbuds, nodding; a round green check badge beside them.
   2.4:1. Parts: (a) the head with both earbuds, no body; (b) the shoulders/upper body alone;
   (c) a green check badge; (d) a lilac cross badge.
   ```
7. **Pause when removed**
   ```
   Scene: a hand lifting an earbud away from an ear; next to it a pause badge. 2.4:1. Parts: (a) an
   ear (side of the head, close-up) without the earbud; (b) the earbud alone; (c) the hand alone,
   fingers as if holding something small.
   ```
8. **Automatic power off**
   ```
   Scene: the earbud resting in its open case on a table, a soft clock/timer ring around them, a
   small power badge. 2.4:1. Parts: (a) the case closed; (b) the earbud; (c) a timer ring; (d) a
   power badge.
   ```
9. **Bluetooth quality**
   ```
   Scene: a phone on the left and the earbud on the right, a stream of soft round dots flowing from
   the phone to the earbud. 2.4:1. Parts: (a) the phone, front view, blank screen; (b) the earbud;
   (c) one big dot and one small dot.
   ```
10. **Voice guidance**
    ```
    Scene: the earbud with three soft sound arcs coming out of it, and a rounded badge with a
    battery symbol. 2.4:1. Parts: (a) the earbud; (b) one sound arc; (c) the battery badge.
    ```

Send the pictures back here, finished picture and parts for each scene. Parts that don't match the
style or the earbuds get asked for again.
